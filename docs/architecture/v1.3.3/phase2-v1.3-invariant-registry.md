## 0. Rules for this registry

Nine rules. Rules 1–5 are v1.1's, retained verbatim in force. Rules 6–8 are new in v1.2, rule 9 is new in v1.3. Rules 6–8 exist and exist because `52 §3` found that **nine of twenty-two BLOCKING defects were a quantity or threshold that the mechanism needs and that the artifact declaring quantities does not declare** — a violation of v1.1's own rule 5, committed by the artifacts written to satisfy it.

1. **One definition per identifier.** No document may redefine an identifier or introduce a new one. A new invariant is added here first.
2. **Where documents cite an identifier inconsistently, this table wins** and the citing document is corrected. Corrections are recorded in `phase2-v1.2-changelog.md`.
3. **Identifiers are never renumbered.** A property cited under a wrong number gets a new identifier, because renumbering is how drift starts. Retirements are recorded in `§4` and the retired identifier is never reused.
4. **Owner is the plane that computes the check.** An invariant owned by the audit plane is not evaluated in the control plane and vice versa. Where both planes evaluate a related property, they are separate identifiers or the row names both.
5. **An invariant with an unstated threshold is not an invariant.** `[ESTIMATE]`-graded thresholds are named as such.
6. **An invariant with an undeclared operand is not an invariant.** Every quantity an invariant reads must exist as authoritative data in a named artifact, and the row names that artifact. *(New in v1.2 — LIM-02: `I3` read a `window ceiling` that no artifact declared.)*
7. **An invariant whose owner cannot observe its required inputs is not an invariant.** The row's Owner must have the inputs in its declared capability specification. *(New in v1.2 — AUD-01: `I17` was owned by a plane whose declared inputs could not evaluate it.)*
8. **An invariant reading a vendor-dependent parameter declares the parameter's provenance** as exactly one of `DOCUMENTED`, `CONFIGURED`, `ESTIMATED`, `MEASURED`, `UNMEASURED`. `UNMEASURED` requires a stated conservative default and an `IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATION_REQUIRED` marker. *(New in v1.2 — SR-S2's cessation parameter, then the now-retired scalar `cessation_lag`; SR-S4's overdelivery multipliers.)*

9. **A printed enforcement expression must contain exactly the operand set its invariant statement requires.** A `CHECK`, a guard predicate or a generated-column definition reproduced in any artifact is compared mechanically against this registry's operand list, and a mismatch is a defect in the artifact, not a variant reading. *(New in v1.3 — TB-01: `24 §3` K5 printed a three-term `window_balance` `CHECK` against a four-term invariant, so the term Mechanism B exists to enforce was enforced by nothing in the artifact declaring the enforcement. Registry rule 2 resolves inconsistent **citation**; it does not repair a schema, and S1 builds the schema from the artifact.)*

**And a method rule for every document that cites this registry, new in v1.2 (SR-R4).** `analysis/v1.1-internal-consistency-pass.md` check C5 verified that every cited identifier **resolves**. It did not verify that the resolved statement **entails the citing sentence**, which is why `I31` was cited four times for a property it does not state. **Check C5b: for every citation, the citing sentence must be entailed by the registry statement.** C5b is run over the whole package before packaging; its output is `analysis/v1.2-consistency-audit.md`.

**Counts, recomputed rather than carried forward, and every one shown rather than asserted.** **70 identifiers** — v1.2's 68 plus `I62` and `I63`. **57** in the MVP set, **13** excluded with named triggers (`§2.7`, `§3`); `57 + 13 = 70`. **23** rows name the audit plane as owner or co-owner — **recounted from `§1`'s owner column in v1.3.1 (E6); v1.3 printed 22, carried forward from v1.2 and not recomputed after `I54`'s owner was restated to name the audit plane for both verification reads.** `I62` and `I63` are both control-plane owned, so neither accounts for the difference. **23** are database-enforced (constraint, trigger, generated column or guard under an explicit lock), **enumerated by name in `§2.8`** so the count is checkable — v1.1 asserted twelve and listed none, and `analysis/v1.1-internal-consistency-pass.md` found arithmetic errors in a registry's own counts.

**Legend — Enforcement:** `DB` = database constraint, trigger, generated column or `CHECK` · `TX` = inside the authorising transaction · `POLICY` = policy-engine evaluation step · `CI` = build-time check · `SCHED` = scheduled continuous check · `RUNTIME` = code assertion on the request path.
**Legend — Slice:** the slice in `37 §2` at which the check becomes live. `T3` = only after real credentials, money or customers exist. `DEF` = deferred with a deferred component; binds when that component lands.

## 2. Notes on the v1.2 changes

### 2.1 `I18`'s replacement, and why it is four rows rather than a tolerance

CAN-01 showed the v1.1 three-way equality is ill-typed. The repair is a type correction, not a relaxation, and it is deliberately four assertions so that **no policy cap intended to bound economic loss can accidentally compare only against `vendor_amount`**:

- the cap is compared against `total_exposure` (`26 §8`, `I18b`);
- the vendor payload is compared against `vendor_amount` (`I18a`);
- the ordering between them is asserted (`I18c`);

## 3. What no invariant establishes

Stated because an invariant table creates confidence in proportion to its length. Six items in v1.1; eight in v1.2; **eleven in v1.3**, and the three additions are all consequences of remediations in this pass.

1. **The hash chain does not prove completeness.** `I41` proves that rows present have not been altered, removed or reordered by a principal holding only `INSERT`. Omission leaves no trace in a chain. `I17` and `I17e` make the chain meaningful for **transport**, and under control-plane compromise they detect divergence and cannot attribute it.
2. **No invariant proves inserted content is true.** A control plane with `INSERT` rights writes plausible false rows freely. Truthfulness comes from `I8`'s independent vendor reads and `I4`'s three-way settlement tie.
3. **`I3` bounds the ledger, not the loss.** `Σ reserved ≤ ceiling` is a property of the reservation table. `Σ settled cost attributable to agent-authorised effects ≤ ceiling` is measurable only at settlement, weeks later. `36 §15`'s T3 gate is the only unforgeable MAL test.
4. **`I15` is empirical and its silence is weak evidence.** A probe that fails to succeed establishes that the vendor refused *that* operation with *that* credential at *that* time.
5. **`I18d`'s settlement leg does not exist until real money moves.** At MVP it is proved against synthetic fixtures, and `58 §13` condition 6 makes persistent divergence a pass-revoking result.
6. **Zero observed violations must always be reported with attempts, attacker budget, adaptivity and sample size.** A finite corpus produces zero trivially.
7. **`I17` and `I17e` do not detect an effect dispatched with no row written on either side** (new in v1.2, AUD-09). Transport completeness is a property of the transport. The only detector for both-sided omission is `I8`, whose own coverage limits are stated on its row.
8. **`I54` is only as strong as its cessation specification** (new in v1.2, SR-S2; restated v1.3, TB-07). Until the four-field specification is **declared** and then measured, `I54` is satisfiable by no adapter, so the conservative default — no `REVOKED` transition, exposure held to the close of the last in-scope window instance — is the operative behaviour and the commercial cost is real, not hypothetical. v1.2's single scalar was not measurable at all, so the S3 obligation gating it was not executable.

9. **No invariant detects a control plane that freezes its own attested prefix** (new in v1.3, TA-01, TJ-01). `I17` and `I17e` establish continuity of the attestation channel and internal consistency and completeness of the **latest attested prefix**. They establish nothing about the true control-journal maximum, because the audit plane has no independent reading of it. A compromised control plane that stays in `NORMAL`, stops pushing at seq 900 while its journal advances to 1,000, and attests `max_journal_seq = 900` with the genuine head hash satisfies `I17`, `I17e`, `I17b`, `I17f(a)` and `I17f(b)` simultaneously and indefinitely. **Two subsets, with different fates.** Rows describing an effect that reached a readable vendor system may be detected by `I8` within `sweep_cadence + vendor_reporting_lag` — after the effect, with the vendor's own record available for recovery, and with `I3`'s authority boundary intact throughout. **Rows describing anything with no vendor counterpart — authorisation decisions, denials, reservations, releases, `PRESUMED_SETTLED` transitions, approvals, resumes, `RemedyObligation`s, state facts, supersessions, escalations, incidents, clock creations, override exercises, `StandingAuthorization` transitions, enumeration reads, `OutOfBandAction` records and the `DISPATCHED_UNMIRRORED` tags themselves — have no detector at all, under any mechanism in this architecture.** That class is large and it is precisely the record of authority use. It is stated here because v1.2 stated the neighbouring case (`§3` item 7, rows omitted before attesting) and not this one.

10. **`I17f(c)` does not close the withheld-row case** (new in v1.3, TA-04). It closes a dispatch inside a stall interval the audit plane **published**. An effect whose row never reaches the mirror is item 9.

11. **`I8` has a declared bound and an unmeasured magnitude** (new in v1.3, TA-02). `sweep_cadence` is `CONFIGURED`; `vendor_reporting_lag` is `UNMEASURED`. **At S1 all four MVP action classes run against mock adapters, so `I8` proves nothing at S1 by construction** and items 9 and 10 have no operative detector at that slice.
