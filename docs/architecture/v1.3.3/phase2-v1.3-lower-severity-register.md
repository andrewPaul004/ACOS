## 2. What this register deliberately does not do

**It does not redesign the non-blockers.** `62 §12` authorises applying the fourteen and scheduling the rest, and the mission is explicit that new architecture mechanisms are out of scope unless a BLOCKING remediation requires a local specification object. TB-13 is the clearest case: a `SUPERSEDED` status with a max-over-instance `standing_cap` is a real design addition with its own attack surface, and inventing it in a verification-only pass — with no fourth adversarial review to catch what it breaks — is worse than disclosing the limitation and scheduling it.

**It does not treat the commercial restriction as a defect.** `26 §10.5` and `51 §3.2` are right that whole-window advertising commitment is a business-model consequence, and `62 §11` condition 3 is the right place for the finding if the measurement returns long. TB-10's remaining items are about the **completeness of the disclosure**, not about the trade.

**It does not silently promote a MATERIAL to a blocker.** Four items carry `S1 BEFORE IMPLEMENTING RELATED COMPONENT` — TB-07, TB-08(a), TB-11, TB-13. **The two count/citation corrections TOS-02 and TOS-03 are no longer scheduled: both are applied in v1.3.1.** **S1 may begin.** What may not begin, per each row, is the specific component: no cessation measurement, no production ceiling seeding on `W_MONTH_ADSPEND`, no `PAUSE_PENDING` retry implementation, and no `campaign.budget.set` supersession path.
