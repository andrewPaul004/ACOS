## 2. Invariant resolution and the C5b entailment check

**Resolution (mechanical).** Every `I\d+[a-f]?` identifier appearing in any deliverable resolves to a row in `phase2-v1.2-invariant-registry.md`. **Unresolved: zero.** All seventeen new identifiers — `I17e`, `I17f`, `I18a`–`I18d`, `I51`–`I61` — are cited in at least three deliverables each. `I18` appears only in historical passages explaining its retirement.

**Entailment (C5b, new in v1.2).** Resolution is not enough: v1.1 passed a resolution check while citing `I31` for a property `I31` does not state. C5b asks, per citation, whether the cited invariant *entails* the property claimed at the citation site.

**Four drifted citations found and corrected**, all the same drift — attributing verify-mode non-increase, or reservation absence, to `I31`'s uniqueness constraint:

| Site | v1.1 citation | Corrected to |
|---|---|---|
| `22 §3` (R9 addition) | *"never re-taken and never topped up (I31)"* | `I31` for non-re-taking, **`I51`** for non-increase — two properties, two invariants |
| `35 §4` | *"denies if it does not (I31)"* | **`I51`**, plus the `RESERVATION_ABSENT` denial for the absent case |
| `36 §2` | *"(I31), and resume in verify mode never tops up"* | Split into two asserted properties; VC-R4 requires them to fail independently |
| `48 §5.1` | *"refused if the reservation has been released or has expired (I31)"* | **`RESERVATION_ABSENT`** — `I31` says nothing about an absent reservation |

The fourth is the most instructive: `I31` was cited for the *opposite* of what it states. A uniqueness constraint on `authorisation_id` guarantees a reservation is not duplicated; it cannot guarantee one exists.

## 4. What this audit does not establish

Stated because a passing self-check is the easiest thing in the package to over-read.

1. **It does not establish that the twenty-two remediations are correct.** It establishes that they are consistently *stated*. `54 §2.1` reproduced every v1.1 figure correctly and v1.1 still contained 22 BLOCKING defects, all semantic.
2. **It does not establish that the mechanical conditions are the right conditions.** They were chosen by the same process that produced the artifacts.
3. **It does not exercise the two mechanisms the third review is scoped to.** Neither the inverted mirror machine nor the standing state machine can be validated by a consistency pass; both are adversarial questions.
4. **C5b was run against the invariant citations, not against every cross-reference.** A `§`-reference pointing at the wrong section would not be caught. Section references were spot-checked, not exhaustively verified.
5. **The SR-S4 deviation is flagged, not validated.** The exposure-remainder form defeats the constructed attack that `57`'s specified form does not; whether it opens a different one is a question for the third review, not for this pass.
