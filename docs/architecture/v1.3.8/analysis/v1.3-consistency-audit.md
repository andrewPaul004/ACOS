# ACOS v1.3 — Internal Consistency Audit

**Run 2026-09-03, after all v1.3 edits, before packaging. This is a self-check on the v1.3 package, not an independent review, and it is labelled as such.**

Executed by `analysis/consistency-v1.3.py` over the full artifact tree. **The script is committed with the package and returns a non-zero exit status on any FAIL**, which is the change `62 §5` asks for: the v1.2 pass reported conditions in prose and two of them returned the wrong answer.

---

## 0. Why this pass is different, and the two false PASSes that made it so (TOS-04)

`analysis/v1.2-consistency-audit.md` returned **PASS** on C5 and C6 and **both were false**.

| Condition | v1.2 text | Returned | Truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| C5 | *"`MAL_total` signature basis stated as $756.00 consistently"* | PASS | `$756.00` appeared in exactly one deliverable; `$600` appeared in two operational passages |
| C6 | *"`Standing(month)` 31-day stated as $186.00 consistently"* | PASS | Two deliverables stated `$180` as standing |

**The word that failed was *"consistently"*.** Both were implemented as *every occurrence of the current figure agrees with every other occurrence of the current figure*, which is trivially satisfied when the current figure appears once and its predecessor appears twice. Neither condition ever looked for the superseded value. The v1.2 audit's `§4` item 2 anticipated that the conditions might be the wrong conditions; it did not anticipate that two would be evaluated wrongly against the right question.

**Three changes make that failure class detectable.**

1. **C5 and C6 are restated** as *"stated identically wherever stated, **and stated nowhere in a superseded form**"*, and the superseded-form leg is implemented as an **explicit denylist of prior-version values**, not as an agreement check over current ones. A denylist is falsifiable in the direction an agreement check is not.
2. **C23 is new** and generalises it: a denylist of superseded *semantic combinations* rather than bare figures, because `$180` is a legitimate current value in fixture F2 (`min(UNBOUNDED, $180.00)`) and a superseded one as a standing basis. The two are distinguished by context, not by the number.
3. **C22 is new** and implements registry rule 9: every printed enforcement expression is compared mechanically against its invariant's declared operand set. That is the condition that would have caught TB-01 in v1.2.

**And the pass now fails.** On its first run against the edited tree it returned **10 PASS / 5 FAIL**. Three were real defects — `26 §10.5` still carrying the retired `cessation_lag` scalar and the referentless *"last referenced window"*; `22 §3.1` and `37`'s falsification list still describing mirror-outage behaviour without the state qualifier; and `48`'s version line displaced by an inserted note. Two were the conditions hitting the ledger's own quotations of the defects it records, and the scan was narrowed to the deliverables, which is the corpus TB-09 is about. **The three real defects were fixed. That first run is recorded here rather than discarded, because a consistency pass that has never failed is indistinguishable from one that cannot.**

## 4. What this audit does not establish

Stated because a passing self-check is the easiest thing in the package to over-read, and because two of v1.2's passed.

1. **It does not establish that the fourteen remediations are correct.** It establishes that they are consistently *stated*, that no printed expression contradicts its invariant, and that no superseded figure survives in an operational passage. `54 §2.1` reproduced every v1.1 figure correctly and v1.1 still contained 22 BLOCKING semantic defects.
2. **It does not re-attack the mechanisms.** `62 §5` authorises a verification-only pass precisely because none of the fourteen introduces a new mechanism. **Three do introduce local specification objects** — `DegradedModeOverride`, `MirrorInputStallSignal` and `standing_window_exposure` — and each was required by a BLOCKING remediation rather than invented. **They have not been adversarially reviewed, and `phase2-v1.3-verification.md §14` records that as the honest residual of running no fourth red team.**
3. **C21's regular expression is a heuristic.** It finds passages that *look like* mirror-outage behaviour statements. A statement phrased so as to match neither the mirror pattern nor the state pattern would pass silently. Manual review covered `22`, `24`, `30`, `34`, `35`, `36`, `37` and `38`; the heuristic covers the rest.
4. **C22 checks one printed guard.** It is the one TB-01 was about and the only four-term enforcement expression printed in the package. A future artifact printing a second one would need the condition extended, and registry rule 9 is the standing obligation to do so.
5. **The denylists are as good as their authors' memory of what was superseded.** C23 carries eight patterns drawn from `60` TB-09 and `62 §5`. A superseded combination neither document named would not be found.
